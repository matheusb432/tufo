The Commons files stand in where no open-access museum scan fit: the open-access dragon paintings were portrait-format or showed nude corpses, the open-access hellscape showed nude figures, and the Met's own cathedral scan is only 1955 px wide. The dragon's largest public-domain scan is 2000 px wide, so its large rendition is smaller than the others'.
