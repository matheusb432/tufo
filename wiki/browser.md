# Browser

Play music and ambience from websites alongside your playlists and soundboards. The browser is in
the sidebar on Linux and Windows, and it only runs while it's open.

**Open the browser** brings back your tabs, and pages keep playing while you use the rest of the
app. The power button closes it and keeps your tabs and logins. It also closes on its own after
ten minutes with nothing playing and its page closed.

The slider next to the title is the volume for all pages. Pages play wherever the rest of the
audio goes, Discord included.

## Limitations

- Pages need a click before they play sound.
- AAC and H.264 aren't supported, and DRM services like Spotify don't play.
- Pages can't use your camera or microphone, download files, or open local files.
- Input methods that compose text before committing it don't work.
- Text copied from a page stays inside the browser.

The browser adds about 375 MB to the installation and uses around 400 MB of memory with a page open.
