# Playback

Play a song from a playlist or **Songs**, and queue up what plays next. Right-click a song and
choose **Add to mix** to layer up to three songs; the **Mix** button next to the title controls
the extra ones. **Recently played**, at the top of Home and under **Recent** in the sidebar, lists
the songs your playlists played last; play one to pick its playlist up again from that song.

Soundboard sounds play over the music. Each has its own volume and can loop for ambience. The
built-in **Effects** board comes with eight ambience loops ([credits](../assets/effects/CREDITS.md)).

With a tray icon, closing the window just hides it. Quitting stops the audio unless **Keep
playing after quitting** is on in Settings.
