# Library

Drop a music folder on **Playlists** or an effects folder on **Soundboards**. Each subfolder with
audio becomes a collection that stays in sync with the disk. Your files are never changed, moved,
or copied.

Use **⊕** to make a collection you fill by hand, with files from anywhere.

Removing a collection never deletes files, and dropping its folder again brings a folder
collection back.

**Ctrl+F** filters the page and **Ctrl+Shift+F** searches everything (**⌘** on macOS). Type
`boss/dragon` to search inside one collection.

Missing files keep their place until you restore them. If you moved your whole library, drop the
new folder.
